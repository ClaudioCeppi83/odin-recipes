Slow Cooker Whole Chicken

Cooking a whole chicken in the slow cooker produces tender, juicy meat with only 5 minutes of prep.

Ingredients:

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 teaspoon lemon pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

Steps:

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season liberally inside and out with salt, pepper, lemon pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Place chicken in the slow cooker and cook 8 hours on Low or 4 to 5 hours on HIGH.
